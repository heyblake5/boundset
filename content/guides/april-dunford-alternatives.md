## Who April Dunford is best for

April Dunford is best for B2B tech teams with real traction and an executive team that needs to align on positioning and a sales pitch. Her workshop puts the whole leadership team in the room with the person who wrote the method. Her site lists clients ranging from Series A startups to global technology companies.

Her workshop fits well when:

- Your leadership team disagrees on why customers choose you
- Marketing and sales tell different stories
- You want the sales pitch built in the same engagement
- You have the budget and can wait a few months for a slot

## Why startups look for alternatives

Startups look for alternatives mostly because of stage and budget. A seed stage team may be too early for her criteria or may not have a five figure budget for a workshop. Some also need positioning settled this month. Some teams also want finished copy, which a workshop does not include.

None of these are knocks on her work. They are fit questions. Her books are a strong starting point for any of the alternatives below.

## The main alternatives compared

The main alternatives are doing it yourself with her books, a positioning sprint, a fractional product marketer and an agency. Each fits a different budget and need. Published prices below were checked in October 2026 on each provider's site.

| Option | Typical format | Published price examples | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Do it yourself with Obviously Awesome | Your team runs the five steps | Price of the book | Teams with time and a strong facilitator inside |
| Positioning sprint | Fixed scope over two to four weeks | Rethink GTM $2,400 (3 weeks). SF Marketing Agency $7,500 (14 business days). Boundset $7,500 or $12,000 (2 weeks) | Startups that need positioning and copy fast |
| Fractional PMM | Part time product marketer for months | Varies, get quotes | Teams that need ongoing launches and enablement |
| Agency | Strategy plus design and production | Varies, get quotes | Rebrands and full site builds |
| April Dunford workshop | Three day facilitated workshop | $50,000 or $80,000 | Growth stage B2B teams aligning leadership |

### Do it yourself with her books
Obviously Awesome lays out her full process. A founder who can run a calm, honest workshop with the team can get far with it. The risk is facilitation. Founders often defend the original idea and find it hard to judge their own product from the outside.

### Positioning sprint
A sprint gives you a fixed scope and an end date. Most use a method close to hers. Sprints differ mainly in deliverables. Some stop at a strategy memo. Others include messaging and homepage copy. The positioning sprint cost guide compares published prices and scope.

### Fractional PMM
A fractional PMM works inside your team part time. It is a good fit when positioning is mostly settled and you need someone to carry it into launches and sales materials every month.

### Agency
An agency handles strategy with design and production. It fits when you need a new brand and site built together. Settle positioning first so the design work does not have to be redone.

For a deeper comparison of the last two, read positioning agency vs fractional PMM.

## FAQ
**Q: How much does April Dunford charge?**
A: Her consultant page lists the Positioning + Sales Pitch Workshop at $50,000 for companies with 10 to 50 people and $80,000 for companies with 50 to 1,000+ people. The larger tier adds advisory sessions. Prices were checked on aprildunford.com in October 2026, so confirm on her site before planning a budget.

**Q: Does April Dunford work with early stage startups?**
A: Her site says she works with growth stage startups and larger organizations and does not work with pre launch or very early release products. She recommends that teams at that stage read Obviously Awesome and work on positioning once they have traction with paying customers.

**Q: What is the April Dunford positioning framework?**
A: Her framework from Obviously Awesome has five components: competitive alternatives, unique attributes, value, best fit customers and market category. You work through them in that order. She starts with alternatives because value only makes sense relative to what a buyer would do instead, and she picks the market category last.

**Q: Can I do April Dunford positioning without hiring her?**
A: Yes. Obviously Awesome lays out her full step by step process, and many teams run it themselves. The hard part is facilitation, because founders often defend their original idea. Free tools and templates help, and many sprints use a similar method if you want outside help.

**Q: How long is the wait to work with April Dunford?**
A: Her contact page says she is generally booked a few months in advance. When checked in October 2026, it said she was booking workshops for Q4 2026. She also notes that she does not sell single paid hours, and points people to her books for common questions.

**Q: What is a cheaper alternative to April Dunford?**
A: A positioning sprint is the most common lower cost option. Published startup sprint prices we checked in October 2026 range from $2,400 to $7,500. Boundset is $7,500 for a two week sprint that includes homepage copy, or $12,000 for Stronghold. Reading her book and running the process yourself costs the least.

**Q: Does Boundset use April Dunford's method?**
A: Boundset's guides and positioning canvas use the five components from Obviously Awesome, credited to her. The sprint adds messaging and copy on top of positioning. That includes a messaging matrix and homepage copy with variants, plus an activation asset such as a cold email sequence.
